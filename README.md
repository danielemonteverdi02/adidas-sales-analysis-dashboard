# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

Interactive sales analysis dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze Adidas sales performance across different years, sales channels, U.S. states, regions, products and retailers.

The main objective of the project is to transform Adidas sales data into an interactive business dashboard and highlight the main patterns in sales and profitability.

---

## Project Overview

The dashboard provides an overview of Adidas sales performance through a combination of KPIs, time-series analysis, geographic analysis and categorical comparisons.

The analysis focuses on:

- Overall sales and profitability
- Monthly sales performance
- Geographic distribution of sales
- Regional sales performance
- Product performance
- Retailer performance
- Sales channels
- Yearly performance

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains Adidas sales data across different U.S. locations, products, retailers and sales channels.

The main information used in the dashboard includes:

- Sales
- Profit
- Units sold
- Price per unit
- Product category
- Retailer
- Region
- State
- Sales channel
- Date

# Dashboard

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard reports the following overall KPIs:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| **Total Sales** | **$899.90M** |
| **Total Profit** | **$332.13M** |
| **Total Units Sold** | **18M** |
| **Average Price per Unit** | **$45.22** |
| **Profit Margin** | **42.30%** |

The business generated approximately **$899.90M in sales** and **$332.13M in profit**, with a total of approximately **18M units sold**.

The resulting profit margin is **42.30%**.

---

### Insights

- **July** was the best-performing month with **$95M** in sales.
- **August** was the second-best month with **$92M**.
- **December** generated **$86M**.
- **March** was the weakest month with **$57M** in sales.
- Sales increased substantially from **March ($57M)** to **July ($95M)**.
- Sales remained relatively high during **July and August**, before declining in September and October.
- The **West** is the strongest region, generating **$269.68M** in sales.
- The **Midwest** recorded the lowest sales among the five regions with **$135.80M**.
- **Men's Street Footwear** is the best-performing product category with **$209M** in sales.
- The difference between the highest-performing category (**Men's Street Footwear**) and the lowest-performing category (**Women's Athletic Footwear**) is approximately **$102M**.
- **West Gear** generated the highest sales with **$243M**.
- **Walmart** recorded the lowest sales among the retailers shown, with **$75M**.
- The three leading retailers — **West Gear, Foot Locker and Sports Direct** — clearly outperform the remaining retailers.
